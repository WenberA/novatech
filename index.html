FAQ

Frequently Asked
Questions

Can I try NovaTech before paying?

Yes — every plan starts with a 14-day free trial, no credit card required, and you can cancel any time.

Can I switch plans later?

Absolutely. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your plan at any time from your account settings.

Does NovaTech integrate with our existing stack?

Yes, NovaTech connects natively with 200+ tools including Slack, Notion, GitHub, Zoom and Google Drive, plus a full API for anything custom.

Is my data secure?

NovaTech is SOC 2 Type II certified with encryption in transit and at rest, and enterprise plans include SSO and custom compliance reviews.

What kind of support is included?

Every plan includes email and community support, with Pro and Enterprise plans getting priority live chat and a dedicated success manager.
